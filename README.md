# ✈️ Airline Performance Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

I recently completed an Airline Performance Analysis project using Power BI to explore airline operations and uncover insights that can support better business decisions.

The dashboard analyzes key performance indicators, including revenue, profit, passenger volume, fuel costs, payment methods, and customer segments.

 ### Key Insights
- $92M Total Revenue
- $57M Total Profit
- $34M Total Cost
- 180K Total Passengers

### What I Discovered
1. Payment Methods: Wallet payments recorded the highest passenger volume, followed by Card, Cash, and Transfer.
2. Airline Profitability: Air France was among the contributors to overall profit.
3. Fuel Costs: Air France and Turkish Airlines recorded notable fuel costs, highlighting the importance of monitoring fuel efficiency.
4. Customer Segments: Passenger and cost patterns varied across Business, Family, Student, and Tourist customers.

### Business Recommendations
Based on the analysis, I recommend that airlines:
1. Focus on profitability and profit margins rather than passenger volume alone.
2. Monitor fuel costs per passenger and per flight to identify opportunities to improve fuel efficiency.
3. Evaluate payment methods and optimize convenient options such as digital wallets and cards.
4. Analyze customer segments to identify the groups that contribute most to profitability.
5. Track profit per flight to evaluate operational efficiency.
6. Introduce route-level and time-based analysis to identify the most profitable routes and travel periods.
